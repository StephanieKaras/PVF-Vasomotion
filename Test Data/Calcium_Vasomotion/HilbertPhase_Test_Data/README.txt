Calcium Vasomotion Hilbert Phase - Test Data

test_01_same_phase.csv
Calcium and diameter oscillate together at 0.10 Hz with approximately the same phase.

test_02_calcium_leads_90deg.csv
Calcium is approximately 90 degrees ahead of diameter.

test_03_calcium_lags_90deg.csv
Calcium is approximately 90 degrees behind diameter.

test_04_opposite_phase.csv
Calcium and diameter are approximately 180 degrees apart.

test_05_changing_phase.csv
The calcium phase gradually changes relative to diameter, providing a simple lower-phase-locking test.

test_06_different_amplitudes.csv
Calcium and diameter have different amplitudes while maintaining an approximately 45-degree phase offset.

Format
Frames are in column D, Time in E, Calcium in F, and Diameter in G. Numeric data begin on row 12. Diameter is percent of baseline 100.
