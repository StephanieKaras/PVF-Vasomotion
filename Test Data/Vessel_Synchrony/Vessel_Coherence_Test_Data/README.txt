Vessel Coherence - Test Data

test_01_high_coherence.csv
Three vessel ROIs with a shared 0.10 Hz oscillation and small independent noise.

test_02_low_coherence.csv
Three vessel ROIs dominated by different frequencies within the vasomotor band.

test_03_phase_shifted_same_frequency.csv
Three vessel ROIs at 0.10 Hz with different phase offsets.

test_04_four_rois_mixed_coherence.csv
Four vessel ROIs with a mixture of shared and different oscillatory frequencies.

test_05_short_recording.csv
A 40-second recording that tests the script's shortened coherence-window behavior.

Format
Frames are in column D, Time in E, and vessel ROI diameter signals begin in column F. Numeric data begin on row 12. Vessel diameter values are percent of baseline 100.
