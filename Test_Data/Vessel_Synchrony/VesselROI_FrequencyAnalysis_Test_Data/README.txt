Vessel ROI Frequency Analysis - Test Data

test_01_same_frequency_rois.csv
Three vessel ROIs oscillating at 0.10 Hz.

test_02_different_frequency_rois.csv
Three vessel ROIs oscillating at 0.06, 0.10, and 0.18 Hz.

test_03_different_amplitudes.csv
Three 0.10 Hz vessel ROIs with different oscillation amplitudes.

test_04_four_rois.csv
Four vessel ROIs with different frequencies across the vasomotor range.

test_05_short_recording.csv
A 70-second recording that tests the shorter 30-second Welch-window branch.

Format
Frames are in column D, Time in E, and vessel ROI diameter signals begin in column F. Numeric data begin on row 12. Diameter is percent of baseline 100.
